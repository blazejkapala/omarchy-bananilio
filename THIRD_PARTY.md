# Country calling codes

Countries.js contains a reduced calling-code/name table derived from Google libphonenumber PhoneNumberMetadata.xml, retrieved 2026-10-13.

Source: https://github.com/google/libphonenumber/blob/master/resources/PhoneNumberMetadata.xml
